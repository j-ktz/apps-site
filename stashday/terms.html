Stashday Pro

Stashday Pro unlocks unlimited funds, the Home Screen widget and CSV export. It's available as:

  • Yearly: $14.99 a year (or the local equivalent). New subscribers get a 7-day free trial. Payment is charged to your Apple Account when the trial ends. The subscription renews automatically each year unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Manage or cancel it in Settings › your name › Subscriptions.
  • Lifetime: $29.99 once (or the local equivalent), for as long as Stashday is available.

If Pro ends, every fund you already have keeps working, including checking off and Paid it. Only adding funds beyond three needs Pro again. Your data is never locked.
