Questions people ask

What is a sinking fund?

Money you set aside a little at a time for a bill you know is coming but don't pay every month, like car registration, holidays, insurance or the vet. When the bill arrives, the money is already there.

How does Stashday work out how much to set aside?

You tell it when you get paid and add your bills with their amounts and due dates. Stashday works out how much each fund needs from every paycheck between now and the due date, and adds them into one number.

Does Stashday connect to my bank?

No, on purpose. You type in your bills once and Stashday does the math. Nothing about your bank ever touches the app.

Which pay schedules does it support?

Weekly, every two weeks, twice a month and monthly.

Is it free?

Yes, for up to 3 funds, with the payday checklist, reminders and a shareable summary. Stashday Pro is $14.99 a year with a 7-day free trial, or $29.99 once, and adds unlimited funds, Home and Lock Screen widgets and CSV export.

Can I use it with YNAB or my budget app?

Yes. Your budget app runs everything. Stashday does one job: the bills that don't come every month. Many people keep both.

Does Stashday collect my data?

No. Your funds stay on your iPhone. The App Store privacy label is Data Not Collected.

Is this financial advice?

No. Stashday does the arithmetic for the bills you enter. For advice about your situation, talk to a financial professional.
