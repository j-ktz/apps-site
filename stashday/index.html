Every bill covered, one paycheck at a time.

Car registration, holidays, insurance, the vet. Stashday tells you exactly how much to set aside each payday so none of them catch you short.

$187

That's the one number to remember. Set it aside, tick each fund off, and when a bill finally arrives, tap Paid it. Stashday logs it and starts saving for the next one.

How it works

  • Tell it when you get paid: weekly, every two weeks, twice a month or monthly.
  • Add what's coming up, or start from ideas like car registration, property tax and holidays.
  • Each payday, check off each fund as you move the money.
  • When a bill is due, tap Paid it. Recurring bills roll forward on their own.

Free, and Stashday Pro

  • Free forever: 3 funds, the payday checklist, reminders, Siri, and a shareable summary.
  • Pro: unlimited funds, Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets with check-off, and CSV export. $14.99 a year with a 7-day free trial, or $29.99 once.

Private by design

No accounts, no bank links, no ads, no analytics. Your funds live on your iPhone and in your own iCloud.
