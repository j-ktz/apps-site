Community Rules

Fantasy Queens is for friends watching together. Keep it that way. Effective October 9, 2026.

What's not okay

Profile photos, names and usernames must not be sexually explicit, hateful, harassing, violent, or impersonate someone else. Don't upload photos you don't have the right to share, or photos of other people without their say-so.

Reporting and blocking

On the Members screen, press and hold a person to report their photo or profile, or to hide their photo for yourself. House Mothers can remove people from their house at any time.

What happens

We review reports within 24 hours. Content that breaks these rules is removed, and accounts that keep breaking them are closed. There is zero tolerance for abusive behavior.

Your account

You can leave a house any time. To delete your account and data, use Delete account in the account menu of the app, or email support@johnkatez.com from the address you signed up with.

Queen photos

Queen photos are shared by the queens or their teams, freely licensed, or used for identification and credited. Any queen or rights holder can ask for a photo to be removed and we hide it within 24 hours. Details at Queen photos.

Contact

support@johnkatez.com. A real person reads every message.
