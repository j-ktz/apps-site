Privacy Policy

Short version: Fantasy Queens collects only what a league needs to work. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Effective October 9, 2026.

What we collect

Account. When you sign in with Apple, Google or email, we receive your email address (Apple may give a private relay address) and a user ID. If you sign in with email and a password, the password is handled by our authentication provider and is never visible to us.

Profile. The display name and @username you choose, an optional profile photo, and an optional Venmo handle if you add one so friends can pay you.

Game activity. Your leagues, picks, votes and when each pick was saved.

Payments you record. If your league tracks dues, a commissioner can mark who has paid. The app doesn't process payments and never holds money.

What we don't collect

No location, contacts, photos or advertising identifier. No analytics or advertising SDKs. We don't track you across other apps or websites, and we don't sell your data.

Who can see it

Members of a league see its members' display names, points and payment status. Your picks are hidden from the league until an episode locks. Votes are hidden until a vote closes. Your @username and photo are shown next to your name. Your Venmo handle is stored with your profile; the app shows it only to a commissioner collecting dues, but it can be read by people in your leagues. Commissioners can remove members. Nobody outside your leagues sees your activity.

Photos, reports and blocking

Profile photos are optional and are stored with Supabase. They're resized on your phone and are visible to people in your leagues. Anyone in a league can report a photo or profile, or hide someone's photo for themselves, from the Members screen. We review reports within 24 hours and remove content that breaks the Community Rules.

Where it is stored

Your data is stored with Supabase, our hosted database and sign-in provider, in the United States. Sign in with Apple and Google are used only to confirm who you are.

Your choices

You can leave a league at any time. To delete your account and data, email support@johnkatez.com from the address you signed up with, and we will delete it within 30 days.

Children

Fantasy Queens isn't directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions: support@johnkatez.com
