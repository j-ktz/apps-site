Queen photos

Fantasy Queens is an unofficial fan app. It is not made by, affiliated with or endorsed by World of Wonder, any network or any of the queens.

Where the photos come from

Each queen's photo shows its source and credit on her card. A photo is one of three kinds: shared by the queen or her team, freely licensed on Wikimedia Commons, or a promotional photo used for identification only. The credit under every photo says which.

Queens and teams: share your photo

If you are a queen, or you represent one, you can send the photo you want shown. Email support@johnkatez.com from an address we can match to you (your booking or management address, or the one on your official page) with:

  • The photo (JPEG or PNG, at least 600 px on the short side).
  • How you want it credited, for example the photographer's name.
  • This sentence: "I own or control the rights to this photo and I give Fantasy Queens a free, non-exclusive license to show it in the app with the credit above. I can ask for it to be removed at any time."

We replace any other photo of you with the one you send, and we keep your email as the record of permission.

Removal requests

If you are a queen, a photographer or another rights holder and you want a photo taken down, email support@johnkatez.com or use Report or request removal on her card in the app. We hide the photo within 24 hours and confirm by email. You do not have to explain why.

Wrong person or wrong credit

Tell us the same way and we will fix it.
