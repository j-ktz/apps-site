Mixed doubles round robin

Updated October 12, 2026. Every schedule on this page was generated by Sitout's own rotation engine.

Short answer

With 4 men and 4 women on 2 courts, play 4 rounds and every man partners every woman exactly once, with every team mixed.

With uneven numbers, someone sits out each round and sometimes a same-gender team is unavoidable. Keep sit-outs fair first and mix teams whenever the players on court allow it.

5 men and 4 women on 2 courts

Nine players on two courts means one sits out each round. While a man sits, 4 men and 4 women are on court and every team is mixed. When a woman sits, 5 men and 3 women are on court, so one team has to be two men.

Which rule wins: fair sit-outs or mixed teams?

You could keep every team mixed by always benching a man when the numbers are off, but then the extra men sit out far more often than everyone else. Over a night that's the bigger unfairness, and it's the one people leave over. A better rule: sit-outs stay even first, and among the players whose turn it is to sit, choose the one that leaves a mixed court. That keeps courts mixed as often as fair sit-outs allow.

How to run a mixed doubles round robin

  1. Count men and women

    Equal numbers that fill your courts (4 + 4 on 2 courts, 6 + 6 on 3) give a perfect mixed night.

  2. Pair every man with a different woman each round

    With 4 + 4, four rounds pair everyone with everyone of the other gender once.

  3. Keep sit-outs fair first

    With uneven numbers, sit out the players with the fewest sit-outs. Between players on the same count, pick whoever keeps the court mixed.

  4. Accept the odd same-gender team

    When the players on court can't all be paired mixed, one team won't be. Say so up front and nobody minds.

  5. Keep score per player

    Each player gets their team's result. Rank by win rate, then point difference per game, then total points, so a player who sat out more isn't penalized for playing fewer games.

  6. Handle late arrivals and early exits

    Someone who arrives mid-night starts level with the group's fewest sit-outs, so they aren't benched round after round to catch up. Someone who leaves keeps every score they already have.

Questions about mixed doubles round robins

How many rounds for 4 men and 4 women?

Four rounds pairs every man with every woman once. Play more rounds for new opponents.

Can a mixed round robin always keep every team mixed?

Only when the players on court include as many men as women. With uneven numbers some rounds can't be fully mixed unless someone sits out more than their share.

What if a player doesn't want to say?

Leave them unassigned. They can partner anyone, and they never count against a mixed pairing.

Does Sitout do mixed doubles?

Yes. Turn on Mixed doubles when setting up the night. Teams are mixed whenever the players on court allow it, and fair sit-outs always come first.
