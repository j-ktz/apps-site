Pickleball rotation for 9 players: who sits out each round

Updated October 11, 2026. Every schedule on this page was generated by Sitout's own rotation engine.

Short answer

With 9 players on 2 courts, 8 play and 1 sits out each round. Play 9 rounds and every player sits out exactly once, plays 8 games, and partners each of the other 8 players exactly once.

With 9 players on 1 court, 4 play and 5 sit out each round. Sit-outs still never differ by more than one between any two players.

9 players on 1 court: 5 sit out each round

With one court, only 4 of the 9 can play, so 5 sit out each round. Over 9 rounds everyone sits out 5 times and plays 4 games. Because more people sit than play, some players sit two rounds in a row. Sit-outs still never differ by more than one between any two players. If you can get a second court, take it: everyone plays 8 of 9 rounds instead of 4.

How to run a 9-player rotation

  1. Use two courts if you can

    With two courts, 8 play and 1 sits out each round. On one court, 5 of the 9 sit out every round.

  2. Write the names down in order

    Sign-up order is fine. The order only breaks ties, so it doesn't matter who goes first.

  3. Pick who sits out

    Each round, the player with the fewest sit-outs sits. On a tie, whoever sat longest ago sits, then whoever is higher on the list.

  4. Give everyone new partners

    Pair each player with someone they haven't partnered yet. The 2-court table above does this for you: in 9 rounds, everyone partners everyone else once.

  5. Keep score per player

    Each player gets their team's result for that game. Rank by win rate, then point difference per game, then total points.

  6. Handle a late arrival

    Someone who arrives mid-night starts level with the fewest sit-outs so far, so they aren't benched round after round to catch up.

Questions about 9-player rotations

How many players sit out with 9 players on 2 courts?

One. Eight players fill two courts and one sits out each round. After 9 rounds, everyone has sat out exactly once.

How many rounds should 9 players play?

Nine rounds is the natural length on 2 courts: everyone sits out once, plays 8 games and partners all 8 other players. If you stop earlier, some players will have played one more game than others, which is why ranking by win rate is fairer than ranking by total wins.

What if a 10th player shows up?

With 10 players on 2 courts, 2 sit out each round and everyone has sat out once after 5 rounds. Have the newcomer start level with the group's fewest sit-outs so they aren't benched to catch up.

Can 9 players use 3 courts?

Not all at once. Three courts need 12 players. With 9 you can fill 2 courts, and the third stays empty.

Is it fair that someone always sits out?

Yes, as long as sit-outs are spread evenly. With the rule on this page, sit-outs never differ by more than one between any two players, and nobody is benched while someone with fewer sit-outs keeps playing.
