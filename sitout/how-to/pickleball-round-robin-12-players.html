Pickleball round robin for 12 players

Updated October 12, 2026. Every schedule on this page was generated by Sitout's own rotation engine.

Short answer

12 players fill 3 courts exactly, so nobody sits out. Play 9 rounds and everyone plays 9 games with 9 different partners.

On 2 courts, 8 play and 4 sit out each round. Over 6 rounds everyone sits out twice, plays 4 games and never repeats a partner.

If you have 13 or 11 players

13 players on 3 courts: 12 play and 1 sits out each round, and it takes 13 rounds for everyone to sit once. 11 players on 2 courts: 8 play and 3 sit, and everyone has sat once after 4 rounds. Either way, a late arrival should start level with the fewest sit-outs so far.

How to run a 12-player round robin

  1. Book 3 courts if you can

    Twelve players fill three courts exactly, so nobody waits. On two courts, a third of the group sits each round.

  2. List the names in sign-up order

    The order only breaks ties between players with the same number of sit-outs.

  3. Give everyone a new partner every round

    The 3-court table above runs 9 rounds without a repeat partner.

  4. Spread opponents too

    Once partners are fresh, pick matchups so players meet different opponents rather than the same two people all night.

  5. Keep score per player

    Each player gets their team's result. Rank by win rate, then point difference per game, then total points, so a player who sat out more isn't penalized for playing fewer games.

  6. Handle late arrivals and early exits

    Someone who arrives mid-night starts level with the group's fewest sit-outs, so they aren't benched round after round to catch up. Someone who leaves keeps every score they already have.

Questions about 12-player round robins

Does anyone sit out with 12 players on 3 courts?

No. Twelve players fill three courts exactly, so everyone plays every round.

How many rounds should 12 players play?

Most nights play 8 or 9 rounds. In 9 rounds on 3 courts everyone gets 9 different partners.

Can every player partner every other player?

A perfect 11-round rotation exists for 12 players, but few nights run that long. Sitout's schedule keeps partners fresh for the first 9 rounds, which covers a typical two-hour session.

How many sit out with 12 players on 2 courts?

Four each round. Everyone sits out once every 3 rounds.

What if a 13th player arrives?

On 3 courts, one player sits out each round from then on. The newcomer joins at the group's fewest sit-outs.
