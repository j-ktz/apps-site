Questions people ask

Who makes these apps?

John Katez, an independent iPhone developer. Each app does one job and has its own support and privacy page.

Do the apps have ads?

No. None of the apps show ads.

What data do the apps collect?

Each app's privacy page says exactly. Stashday, Statecount, Sitout and Tabbed collect no data. Music Video Night has no accounts, analytics or servers. Mackro uses anonymous analytics and sends only what you submit for AI logging. Fantasy Queens collects only what a house needs to work.

How much do they cost?

Every app is free to download. Some offer an optional upgrade, one-time or yearly, with the price shown in the app before you buy.

Are the apps on Android?

Not yet. They're made for iPhone, and some also run on iPad.

How do I get help?

Email support@johnkatez.com, or open the Support page for the app you're using.
