Count your days in every state

Statecount keeps a day-by-day record of which US state or country you were in, and warns you before you reach a limit you set, like 183 days.

Your location never leaves your phone. The state or country is worked out on your iPhone. No account, no servers, no analytics. Data Not Collected.
Every day, with its source. Each record shows whether it came from your phone's location or was entered or edited by you. Days with no record are flagged so you can fill them in.
Limits and alerts. Days used and days left for every limit, with alerts at 30, 14 and 7 days left.
An audit-ready report. Export every day of the year as a PDF and CSV.
